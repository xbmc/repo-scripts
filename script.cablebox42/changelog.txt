  a show's own Promo / Trailer clips from its Extras folder, and summer reruns

- Fixed: tuning into some DTS-HD programmes partway in froze the picture and
  left Kodi without sound until Kodi restarted (also in Kodi's own player
  afterwards). Kodi 21 can misread the first DTS frame when a file is opened
  at a position (Planet Earth II S01E03 at 19:05-19:07, every time); CableBox
  now opens DTS files with passthrough at the start and jumps to the position,
  which never got stuck (N2: 158 fast channel changes, 65 DTS starts, 40 starts
  at the bad spots). If Kodi's sound output gets stuck anyway, CableBox notices
  within a second: with the TV on it restarts Kodi and comes back on the same
  channel; with the TV off it asks (nothing inside a running Kodi clears it)

  sounds backup forgotten: two saves of CableBox's state file at once (as when
  the TV comes on) could leave it empty. Saves now take a lock, change only
