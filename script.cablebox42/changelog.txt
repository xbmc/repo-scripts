v1.1.0 (2026-11-04) - everything since 1.0.0

Broadcast networks and stations
- Three broadcast networks on 3, 4 and 5 with a network-style day: morning news,
  daytime game shows, afternoon soaps, after-school cartoons, prime time and
  late-night talk, Saturday morning cartoons and a Sunday movie
- Shows go to the station for the network they aired on (NBC to Keystone,
  Nickelodeon to Chameleon, PBS to PES...), and a network's own shows never air
  on another network
- Built-in fictional stations with their own logos and call letters for most
  channels; weather on 25, news on 42
- Branding by time of day: a channel can go by another name and logo in a time
  block (CAM Jr. in the mornings, Cam O Nite at night)
- Station logo as a translucent "bug" in a corner during shows and in the banner

Station folders (your own logos, bumps, station IDs and commercials)
- One folder per channel, plus a folder per time block, in your Stations folder
- Channel editor > Branding & clips: the logo, bumps, station IDs and
  commercials a channel really uses, where each comes from, and a warning when
  clips sit in a folder that is switched off for the channel; makes, repairs or
  browses the channel's folders, or adopts an unused one
- A channel no longer picks up another channel's folder (such as
  "Disney Showcase 13" for channel 3): folders match the channel's name, call
  letters or number ("7", "Channel 7", "CH 7") exactly
- Settings > Tools > Station folders makes only what's missing, reuses existing
  folders, and reports which folder each channel uses and which no channel uses
- Renaming a channel (or its call letters or station) offers to rename its
  folder too - never overwriting anything

Finding the right channel for every show
- Channel manager > Shows without a good channel (also on the Tools page):
  shows no channel picks up, and shows that only just fit where they are, each
  with the reason ("Western (main genre) -> Tumbleweed TV", "No channel's
  programming includes it"); put a show on a channel, mark it as fine, or keep
  it off a channel or off the air
- Lineup summary after the lineup builder, and any time from the Channel
  manager: every channel, where its shows come from, its folders, next steps

Closed captions
- CC on or off for every programme, like a TV's CC setting: main menu > Closed
  captions, or the Menu button while watching
- Caption options while a programme is on: choose the track, load a subtitle
  file, search online (without pausing live TV)
- Captions stay on through commercial breaks and channel changes, and come back
  within moments of a channel change
- Sidecar subtitle files (Movie.en.srt, Movie.en.forced.srt) are preferred over
  a rip's own tracks, named and ranked the way Kodi reads them
- Blu-ray rips: forced subtitles for foreign-language scenes show on TV boxes;
  captions off keeps the forced track
- Broadcast (CEA-608) captions picked up when they appear a few seconds in
- TV boxes (CoreELEC, Android, Raspberry Pi): while captions are on, programmes
  play in Kodi's full-screen player with CableBox's banner, logo and channel
  number on top; CoreELEC subtitles inside the file now appear
- Captions switched from a phone remote (Yatse, Kore) or Kodi's own menus count
- "CC" in the channel banner for programmes that have captions
- Settings > Tools > Remote CC button lets the remote's subtitle key work
- A one-time tip in the TV points to the Menu button for captions and more

The TV itself
- Turn-on and turn-off effect with sound (Settings > Viewing); the turn-off
  sound is heard in full, with the programme silenced first
- Static between channels: Automatic, Quick or Video clip (fixes the sound on
  Windows)
- Stays in the TV window, with no loading wheel when changing channel
- Weather Channel: its own retro icons; the city you type wins over Kodi's
  weather location
- Setup asks for guide and weather music
- Disc images, Blu-ray folders and .strm files now work fully: captions, full
  screen, and the static clears as soon as the picture is on
- Fixed: after the guide or weather channel, changing channel could lose the
  subtitle and audio track lists in Kodi, Yatse and Kore

Documentation
- HOWTO.md: step-by-step guide from installing to building your own stations
- Help explains captions, station folders and the new tools

v1.0.0 (2026-10-31) - first release
- Builds a cable TV lineup from your Kodi library: genre, decade, studio and
  movie channels, one home channel per show
- Shows air on a real clock with commercial breaks, bumps and station IDs from
  your own folders
- Program guide channel and weather channel with background music
- Weekly planner for pinned time slots, automatic prime time, overnight
  sign-off with a "Please Stand By" card
- Holiday programming for Halloween, Thanksgiving and Christmas
- Short programmes packed into one time slot; CH 03 channel number and
  optional CRT effect
- Watch progress protection, AV1 and broken-file handling
