v 4.0.0
- new sign-in with a code on simkl.com/pin or a QR code for the phone (the old PIN login could hang forever); existing logins keep working, reconnect from the settings
- real-time tracking: start, pause and stop are sent as they happen, the position is kept on Simkl when you stop early
- watched marks made in the Kodi library are mirrored on Simkl; a single unmark removes an episode from the history, for a movie you are asked (remove, Plan to watch or keep)
- sync from Simkl into the Kodi library: watched marks, by hand or on a schedule; optional resume positions from other Simkl apps
- rewatch logging for Simkl Pro or VIP accounts
- Simkl entry in the context menu: mark as watched, remove from history, add to Plan to watch, rate, sync updates from Simkl
- ratings: a 1-10 prompt after a movie (optional), and ratings set in Kodi's information dialog go to Simkl
- a watch that could not be sent (offline, Simkl down) is kept and sent later
- exclusions (Live TV, HTTP, add-ons, folders), trailers and clips are skipped, specials are no longer sent as movies
- YouTube and other items without ids are no longer guessed by title
- new settings page
